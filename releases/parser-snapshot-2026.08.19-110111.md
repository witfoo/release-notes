## Summary

This release refines the VMware ESXi parser as part of an ongoing audit effort, adding identification support for two additional ESXi service processes and improving classification accuracy for log entries containing partial binary content. The total parser collection stands at 305 parsers.

## What's New

### Features

- **VMware ESXi — New service identifiers:** Added fingerprint recognition for `clomd-whatif` and `sfcb-CIMXML-Processor` process identifiers, enabling proper attribution of log entries originating from these ESXi subsystems.

### Improvements

- **VMware ESXi — Binary payload classification:** Widened the printable-fragment matching window in the `binary_payload` classifier, reducing false negatives for log lines that contain mixed binary and human-readable content. This ensures more ESXi log entries are correctly categorized rather than dropped or misclassified.
